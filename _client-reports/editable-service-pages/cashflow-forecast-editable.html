FAQ

Questions founders ask before booking.

How accurate is a 13-week cashflow forecast?
Accurate enough to drive real decisions. The first 4–6 weeks are typically within a few percent because most receipts and payments are already booked. The latter half flexes more, which is why we run scenarios.
Why 13 weeks specifically?
13 weeks is one quarter — long enough to spot tight points and plan around them, short enough that the forecast stays grounded in actual receivables and payables rather than guesses.
Do you work with my existing accounting system?
Yes — we typically plug into Xero, QuickBooks, NetSuite, or whatever you run on. The forecast is built around your real chart of accounts and historic patterns.
How is this different from the report my accountant produces?
A standard cashflow statement looks backward. This is forward-looking, weekly, and decision-oriented. It is about what cash is going to do, not what it has done.
How is pricing structured?
Scope-based monthly retainer. Agreed after the initial review based on cadence and complexity. Rolling month-to-month — no lock-in.
Can the forecast include scenarios for fundraising or M&A?
Yes. The base/best/worst structure is built for exactly this. We model funding rounds, acquisitions, exit scenarios, or simply "what happens if we delay this hire by a quarter" — and the impact on cash is visible immediately.
What if my data is messy in Xero / QuickBooks?
Realistically, it usually is at this stage — that's part of why founders engage us. The first two weeks include a light clean-up of the chart of accounts so the forecast is built on data you can trust. We do not require perfect data to start.
Does the forecast cover both UK and international cash?
Yes — we model multi-entity and multi-currency where it matters. The 13-week view consolidates all bank accounts and currencies into a single base-currency picture, with the option to drill down per entity.
